# ATLAS digest — 2026-10-10

## [EXOT] Search for charged lepton flavour violation (e-mu) in top quark decays
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01308
Final state: Three charged leptons (electrons and/or muons) — specifically an oppositely-charged electron-muon pair from the cLFV vertex plus one additional lepton from a SM top-quark decay — accompanied by at least one jet (b-tagged) and missing transverse energy from the neutrino.

Models tested: Charged lepton flavour violation (cLFV) in the e-μ sector via dimension-six two-quark two-lepton EFT operators (Warsaw basis), covering scalar, vector, and tensor Lorentz structures for both u-quark and c-quark couplings to the top quark (six Wilson coefficients in total), interpreted in the context of single-top cLFV production and top-quark pair production with a cLFV top decay.

Key result: No significant deviation from the Standard Model is observed, and 95% CL upper limits are set on six Wilson coefficients ranging from |C^tensor_{eμtu}|/Λ² < 0.018 TeV⁻² to |C^scalar_{eμtc}|/Λ² < 0.32 TeV⁻², corresponding to upper limits on cLFV top-quark decay branching ratios of B(t → eμu) < 6×10⁻⁹ and B(t → eμc) < 2.7×10⁻⁷.

## [HMBS] Compressed SUSY search with two tau-leptons
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37215
Final state: At least one hadronically decaying τ-lepton plus either one additional charged lepton (electron or muon) or one or more additional hadronically decaying τ-leptons, accompanied by missing transverse energy and optionally one ISR jet.

Models tested: R-parity-conserving simplified SUSY models with compressed mass spectra, including direct τ-slepton (stau) pair production, chargino pair production, and associated chargino–neutralino production, all with cascade decays through intermediate τ-sleptons or τ-sneutrinos to a neutralino LSP.

Key result: Results are broadly consistent with the Standard Model (with local excesses of 2.1–2.4σ that weaken observed limits), and exclusion limits at 95% CL are set for Δm(τ̃, χ̃₁⁰) = 30 GeV up to τ-slepton masses of 115 GeV in the ISR channel alone, for Δm(χ̃₂⁰, χ̃₁⁰) = 30 GeV up to χ̃₂⁰ masses of 200 GeV, and for chargino masses up to 175 GeV at the same mass splitting, while no combined exclusion on direct stau production is set due to the observed excesses.

## [HMBS] VBS VVh search, Run 2
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25153
Final state: Two same-sign charged leptons (e or μ), missing transverse momentum (E^miss_T), a boosted Higgs boson candidate reconstructed as a single large-radius jet (H→bb̄), and two forward VBS jets

Models tested: Standard Model electroweak W±W±Hjj production via vector boson scattering; anomalous quartic WWHH coupling parameterized by the coupling modifier κ_WW

Key result: No significant excess over the Standard Model prediction was observed, and observed (expected) values of κ_WW outside the interval −2.1 < κ_WW < 4.1 (−1.9 < κ_WW < 3.9) are excluded at 95% confidence level.

## [EXOT] HQT - Multitops (3/4 tops) (Run 2+3)
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24950
Final state: Exactly two same-sign leptons (electrons or muons) or at least three leptons, produced in association with multiple top quarks (three or four top quarks in the final state)

Models tested: Simplified top-philic resonance model featuring a colour-singlet vector boson (Z') coupled exclusively to top quarks, parameterised by coupling strength c_t and chirality angle θ (spanning left-handed, right-handed, and mixed coupling scenarios)

Key result: No significant excess above the Standard Model background was observed, and a top-philic Z' boson with mass below 1150 GeV is excluded at 95% confidence level for the benchmark scenario with c_t = 1 and θ = 0, compared to an expected exclusion limit of 1250 GeV.

## [EXOT] Photon + Jet resonance search
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26935
Final state: One high-transverse-momentum photon and one jet (γ + jet), with events containing electrons or muons vetoed, examined via the γ-jet invariant mass spectrum.

Models tested: Excited quark compositeness models (light-flavour q*_L (u*/d*), heavy-flavour b* and c*) with varying couplings f; Quantum Black Holes (QBH) in the Randall-Sundrum type-I (RS1) extra-dimension model (n=1) and the Arkani-Hamed-Dimopoulos-Dvali (ADD) model (n=6); generic Gaussian-shaped resonances for model-independent reinterpretation.

Key result: No significant excess above the Standard Model background was observed, allowing exclusion at 95% CL of excited quark masses up to 6.0 TeV (q*_L), 3.5 TeV (c*), and 2.6 TeV (b*) for f=1, and quantum black hole mass thresholds up to 5.3 TeV (RS1) and 7.5 TeV (ADD), with the c* limits representing the first such constraints in the γ-jet final state at the LHC.

## [HMBS] Search low-mass ditau resonances in association with top quark pairs
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28484
Final state: One electron or muon (from semileptonic tt̄ decay) + boosted hadronic di-tau pair (both tau leptons reconstructed in hadronic decay mode as sub-jets within a single large-radius jet) + b-jets, with missing transverse energy implied by leptonic top decay

Models tested: Two-Higgs-Doublet Model (2HDM) with a light pseudoscalar resonance *a* (20–85 GeV) produced in association with a top-antitop quark pair and decaying to τ⁺τ⁻; also interpreted in a model-independent framework covering broader BSM extended Higgs sector scenarios including dark matter models, neutral naturalness, first-order electroweak phase transition models, composite-Higgs models, dilaton, and axion-like particle scenarios

Key result: No significant excess over the Standard Model background was observed, and model-independent upper limits of 0.19 fb on the visible cross-section at 95% CL were set, along with exclusion limits on σ(tt̄a) × BR(a→ττ) ranging from 0.4 pb at m_a = 20 GeV to 0.05 pb at m_a = 85 GeV within the 2HDM signal scenario.

## [EXOT] Exotic pair-produced VLQ T->St decays
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28381
Final state: Two photons (from S → γγ), at least one b-jet, large scalar sum of transverse momenta of jets/leptons/photons plus missing transverse momentum (p_T^vis + E_T^miss), with possible additional leptons or jets from the second T decay chain
Models tested: Pair-produced vector-like T quarks (VLQs) decaying via T → St, where S is an exotic (pseudo)scalar decaying to γγ, Zγ, WW, ZZ, or tt̄; motivated by partial compositeness and composite Higgs scenarios
Key result: No significant excess over the Standard Model background is observed, and upper limits on the fiducial cross-section times branching ratio are set as a function of VLQ mass (1000–1800 GeV) and spin-0 particle mass (200–1200 GeV), representing the first VLQ search performed in this final state.

## [EXOT] Search for high mass dilepton resonances
Link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.28334
Final state: Two same-flavour, opposite-sign leptons (ee or μμ), fully reconstructed dilepton pair with invariant mass > 130 GeV, no missing energy requirement.

Models tested: Sequential Standard Model Z′ (Z′_SSM), E6 GUT-motivated Z′ bosons (Z′_χ and Z′_ψ), and a generic model-independent spin-1 resonance with various width hypotheses.

Key result: No significant deviation from the Standard Model background was observed, and combining Run 2 (140 fb⁻¹ at 13 TeV) with Run 3 (165 fb⁻¹ at 13.6 TeV) data, lower mass limits of 5.5 TeV, 5.1 TeV, and 4.8 TeV were set at 95% CL on the Z′_SSM, Z′_χ, and Z′_ψ models respectively, representing the most stringent limits to date on these high-mass dilepton resonances.

## [EXOT] HNL t-channel (Tau)
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27307
Final state: Same-sign τ lepton pairs plus an additional lepton (ττ, eτ, or μτ same-sign pairs) produced in association with two jets via vector boson scattering
Models tested: Heavy Majorana neutrino (HNL) model with t-channel production via vector boson scattering, probing lepton-number-violating mixing with third-generation leptons (|V_τN|², |V_eN|², |V_μN|²)
Key result: No significant excess above the Standard Model background was observed, and constraints on |V_τN|² were set for heavy Majorana neutrino masses between 92 GeV and 6.5 TeV, improving existing limits above 1.0 TeV and extending exclusion into the previously unexplored mass range above 1.5 TeV.

## [HMBS] Inclusive search for Higgs to dark photons
Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14362
Final state: One photon (γ) plus missing transverse momentum (p_T^miss)

Models tested: Higgs boson decay to a massless dark photon (H → γγ_d) via an effective loop vertex with BSM messenger fields, motivated by dark sector U(1)_D models; also broadly applicable to very low-scale SUSY breaking (gravitino/bino decays), NMSSM Peccei–Quinn limit, and right-handed neutrino scenarios

Key result: No significant excess is observed over the Standard Model prediction, and a 95% CL observed (expected) upper limit on B(H → γγ_d) of 1.4% (1.2%) is set using 135 fb⁻¹ of ATLAS Run 3 data, improving to 0.9% (0.9%) when combined with previous Run 2 results.
